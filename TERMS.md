## What the app does

OpenLocate makes an iPhone or iPad report a location you choose instead of its real one,
using the developer features Apple builds into iOS. It needs the device connected by cable,
unlocked, trusted, and with Developer Mode switched on.

## Other apps may forbid it

Many apps and games forbid faking your location in their terms of service. Pokémon GO and
other location-based games are well-known examples, and so are some dating, delivery and
ride apps. They can detect it and may suspend or permanently ban your account. Read the terms
of every app you use while a fake location is active. Any warning, suspension, ban or lost
purchase is your risk alone.

## Effects on your device

While a fake location is active, every app on the device sees it, including Find My, Maps,
Weather, emergency features and the clock's time zone. After you stop, the device can keep
showing the old place for a while; turning Wi-Fi off and on, or restarting the device,
brings the real location back. Developer Mode lowers some of the device's security
protections; switch it off in Settings when you no longer need it. Do not rely on a device
with a fake location for navigation or in an emergency.

The map, the address search and the routes come from free community services (see

real-world navigation. Use them fairly: they are shared with everyone and are not meant for
heavy or automated use.
